How to scan a document and extract text offline on your phone

Have a contract, receipt or letter and need the text inside it? You can do it from your phone in a minute, without uploading the page to any website. These steps use Timaxo Scan, a web app that reads text on your device itself.

Steps

  1. Open Timaxo Scan in your browser, or add it to your home screen. After the first open it also works without internet.
  2. Tap the camera and photograph the page, or import an existing photo from your device.
  3. Check the page edges. The app detects them automatically, and you can adjust them by hand.
  4. Enhance the image if the light is poor or the page is tilted.
  5. Choose “Extract text” and pick the language (Auto, Arabic or English). Recognition runs on your device.
  6. Review the text, fix any wrong character, then copy it or export it in the format you need.

Tips for better accuracy

  • Shoot in good light, with no shadow across the page.
  • Keep the page flat and photograph it straight from above.
  • Move closer until the text fills the frame, without losing sharpness.
  • If the page mixes Arabic and English, leave the language on Auto.

Why no upload?

When recognition runs on your device, the page never leaves it. That matters for sensitive papers such as contracts and IDs. The only exceptions are if you choose online translation or saving to Google Drive yourself.
