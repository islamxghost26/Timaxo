Scan your documents. Extract the text. Keep your privacy.

Timaxo Scan is a web app that runs on your phone: it photographs the page, finds its edges automatically, and reads Arabic and English text on the device itself.

Open the appHow do I install it?

Features

  • Arabic and English text recognition (OCR) runs on your device
  • Automatic page edge detection and image enhancement
  • Works offline after the first open
  • No accounts and no ads
  • Translation on-device, or online by your choice and after your consent
  • Encrypted backup, plus optional saving to your own Google Drive

Install it on your phone (no app store)

  • Android (Chrome): tap ⋮ then “Install app” or “Add to Home screen”.
  • iPhone (Safari): tap the Share button then “Add to Home Screen”.

Privacy

Your documents are never sent to any server of ours. The only exceptions are if you choose online translation or saving to Google Drive yourself. Read the privacy policy.
